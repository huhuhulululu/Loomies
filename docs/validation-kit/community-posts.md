# 社区触达草稿（ToS-safe）

> **硬规则**：绝不在任何竞品自有社区发招募/问卷。r/femalefashionadvice 严限调研——**先私信 mod 求批准**，否则被公开揭穿马甲会毒化真上线（11 号报告 §6.3）。披露真名、无链接（除非对方要）、只问不卖。

## 渠道 1：Reddit（需 mod 批准）

主：r/femalefashionadvice。备（更容忍研究）：r/CapsuleWardrobe、r/AskWomenOver30。

**先发 mod 私信**：
> Hi mods — I'm [真名], an independent builder doing pre-build research (not selling anything, no link, no product). I'd like to ask the community how they actually decide what to wear in the morning. Is a disclosed, no-link discussion post allowed, or do you prefer I use a specific format/flair? Happy to follow your rules.

**批准后的帖（无链接）**：
> Indie builder here ([真名]), doing pre-build research, nothing to sell and no link. I'm trying to understand how you all actually decide what to wear on a busy work morning. If you're up for it: what actually makes a morning hard, and what have you tried (a routine, a system, an app) to make it easier? What stuck and what didn't? Genuinely just listening.

## 渠道 2：独立职场/胶囊 newsletter（Substack/beehiiv）

作者自有渠道，需明确同意。给作者一段可直接插入 issue 的话：
> A quick favor: [真名], an independent builder (not a big brand, nothing to sell), is researching how working women handle the daily "what do I even wear" problem before building anything. If you have 4 minutes, the anonymous survey is here: [问卷链接]. No signup, no pitch.

Elpha 用指定的 feedback/research 版块：

## 渠道 4：竞品相邻中立讨论（"2026 该用哪个衣橱 App"帖）
