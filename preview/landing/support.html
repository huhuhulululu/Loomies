In the app

Me → Help & FAQ covers what leaves the device, why a recommendation can be empty, and how to delete everything. Suggestions stay yours to override — the app does not make the final call.

Diagnostics

Me → Support → Export diagnostics produces a file of counts and recent logs, with closet names and city removed. This build does not publish a public inbox; when one exists it will be listed in this section.

Policies

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use live in the app under Me → Help. The same text is published as static pages next to this one.
