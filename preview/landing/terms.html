What Loomies does

Loomies suggests outfits from the pieces you add and shows them on a body-shape avatar. Suggestions are advice, not a fitting guarantee — you always make the final call, and every recommendation can be overridden.

Fit marks and avatars

Fit marks are computed from the flat measurements you enter and are an approximation, not a promise that a garment will fit. The avatar is a proportion guide built from a body-shape catalog; it is not a photo-realistic try-on of your own body.

Your content

The photos and text you add stay yours. Because they are stored on your device, keeping backups is up to you — deleting the app or the data removes them permanently.
