What makes it different

The four things every other app gets wrong, done right.

It makes the call, so you never have to

Every morning it settles on one full look from your own closet for the real weather, the daytime kind, not last night's low, and for whatever your calendar holds. No options to weigh, no decisions to make. It won't repeat yesterday's sweater, and it won't pair two blazers. You just wear what's waiting.

Honest fit, from your measurements

Add your measurements once and it flags where a piece runs tight or loose on your actual proportions, in plain words. It describes the clothing, never your body.

Knows where the off-season stuff went

When your winter coat is boxed for the season, it tells you which bin, instead of pretending the coat vanished.

No item cap, no server that can lose your closet

Add everything you own for free. Your clothes and measurements live on your device and your own iCloud, not on a startup's server. A one-time purchase option, not a subscription trap.
